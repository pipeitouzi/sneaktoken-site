    What these calculators deliberately do not do

    They do not guess your token mix. A chatbot and a document pipeline with identical spend have completely different input/output shapes, and any default we picked would be wrong for one of them.

    They do not model quality. Cheapest-per-token is not cheapest-per-solved-problem — if a cheap model needs three retries, the retry tokens are on your bill too.

    They do not include the costs that never appear on a pricing page: the engineering week to stand up vLLM, the on-call rotation, the eval suite you need before you can safely change a model version. The ops-overhead field exists because that number is real, and it is usually bigger than people expect.

    One honest limitation: throughput is the input that swings the break-even result hardest, and it is the one almost everybody overestimates. Measure it on your own workload before you believe the answer.
