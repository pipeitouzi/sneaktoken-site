How the watch works

On each pass we re-read every provider's own pricing page and store the result with a timestamp. The next pass is compared against the stored copy, and only differences are published — which is how you notice a 20% cut that was never announced.

When a page cannot be reached, nothing is overwritten. The row keeps its last known value and its old date rather than being blanked. That is why some entries here are older than others: the date is the honest part.

We do not take numbers from aggregators, model catalogues or blog posts, including when our own fetch fails. If we could not read the official page, the cell stays empty. A blank is information; a wrong number is not.

Corrections: if you spot a price that disagrees with the provider's page, email pipeitouzi@gmail.com. We re-check and date the correction, and we log the change rather than quietly editing history.
