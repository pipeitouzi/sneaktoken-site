Why this site exists

Three numbers decide your bill. Most comparisons quote one.

01

Output is the expensive half

Output tokens routinely cost 3-5x input. A model that wins on input price can lose by a mile on a workload that generates long answers. Compare output first.

02

Cached input is a different product

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash charges $0.30/M for input and $0.006/M for a cache hit — a 50x gap. If your prompt has a stable prefix, the headline price is irrelevant.

03

The same model has several prices

GLM-5.3 is $1.40/$4.40 hosted by Mistral and by Together — and the two differ on cached input. Neither number is "the" price; both are real, for a specific channel.
